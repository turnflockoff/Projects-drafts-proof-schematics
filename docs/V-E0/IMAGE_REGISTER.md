## Evidence classification

- **Original evidence** means photographs supplied in the project conversation. These show physical items but do not, by themselves, prove electrical function, pinout, or operation.
- **Generated concept** means AI-generated visual material created during design exploration. It is not an original hardware photograph, measured CAD, verified schematic, or manufacturing-ready design.
- Dates remain blank unless directly established. Filename sequence is not treated as a date.

## C. What this image set supports

The original photographs support the following limited claims:
1. A physical scooter electronics assembly and multiple green PCBs were photographed.
2. A rear connector panel with nine colored ports was photographed.
3. A display assembly and enclosure were photographed.
4. A battery label was photographed; visible text appears to show 46.8 V, 15 Ah, 702 Wh.
5. Several board/module labels and test-point labels were previously transcribed from close-up photos, but each transcription should be rechecked against original-resolution images before design use.

The photos do **not** by themselves establish:
- Exact connector pinout, polarity, voltage, current rating, or signal direction.
- Data-bus protocol, baud rate, message format, or authentication.
- Battery chemistry, cell count, maximum charge voltage, or BMS limits.
- Compatibility with a specific motor controller.
- That any generated replacement board will fit or function.
